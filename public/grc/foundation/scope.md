# Ashgrove Dental Practice Ltd: engagement scope (fictional)

**Company:** Ashgrove Dental Practice Ltd, two clinics in Bristol (Clifton and Redland). Founded 2011.
**Size:** 21 current staff (6 dentists, 3 hygienists, 7 dental nurses, 3 reception, 2 management/finance) plus an external IT contractor.
**Patients:** about 6,500 registered patients.
**Data:** patient records including medical history and treatment notes (health data), X-ray images, appointment lists, payment details, staff records.
**Systems:** Microsoft 365 (email, files), a cloud practice management system, a cloud X-ray image store, an SMS appointment-reminder service, clinic PCs, printers.
**Regulation:** UK GDPR (health data is special category data), and the practice is inspected by the care regulator.

## Engagement
- **You are:** an outside assessor doing a short review for the owners, who want to know where they stand before an inspection.
- **Three topics:** (1) who has access to what, (2) how security incidents are handled, (3) how suppliers who hold patient data are managed.
- **Audit period:** 1 Jan to 30 Sep 2026. Data exports were taken 14 Oct 2026.
- **Evidence:** in `data-room/`. Ask the client (Jess Stone, practice manager) for anything else, in chat.
- **Out of scope:** backups, physical security, the privacy notice, and staff training.
- **Method:** compare what the practice's own policies say with what the evidence shows. Use only rules written in the practice's policies. There are no outside standards in the data room.

## Your output
Eight findings in the five-C format, each rated, plus one "can't test" note. Read `my-work/README.md` for the order of work. There are more things you could write up than slots, so choose the eight with the strongest evidence.
