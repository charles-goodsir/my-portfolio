# Supplier Policy v1.0

Written: January 2025. Owner: Practice Manager.

1. No personal data is shared with a supplier until a written data processing agreement (DPA) is in place.
2. Critical suppliers (those that hold patient records or images) are reviewed every 12 months, and the review is recorded.
3. The Practice Manager keeps the supplier register up to date.
