# Access Control Policy v2.0

Owner: Practice Manager (J. Stone). Approved by the owners: 9 January 2026. Next review: January 2027.

1. Every member of staff has their own individual account. A shared login is allowed only with written approval from the Practice Manager, recorded in the exceptions register.
2. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be switched on for every account that can open patient records.
3. When someone leaves the practice, their account is disabled by the end of their last working day.
4. Account access is reviewed every six months by the Practice Manager. The signed review is filed.
5. Passwords are at least 10 characters. Accounts lock after 5 failed sign-ins.
6. Staff can open the records only of patients booked at their own clinic. Access to patient records is logged.
