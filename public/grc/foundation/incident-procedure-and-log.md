# Incident Procedure v1.1

Written: March 2025. Owner: Practice Manager.

1. Anyone who spots a security problem (suspicious email, lost device, wrong recipient, odd login) tells the Practice Manager (J. Stone) straight away.
2. The Practice Manager records it in the incident log and takes immediate action.
3. If patient data may be affected, the Practice Manager decides within 72 hours whether the ICO must be told and whether patients must be notified, and records the decision in the log.
4. The Practice Manager reviews every incident within 5 working days and records the cause and any change made.
