# Halden & Moss LLP: engagement scope (fictional)

**Company:** Halden & Moss LLP, a small accountancy practice in Leeds. Founded 2008.
**Size:** 15 staff: 3 partners, 4 accountants, 2 bookkeepers, 2 trainees, 2 admin, 1 receptionist, 1 IT lead (see interview notes).
**Clients:** about 300 small businesses and sole traders.
**Data:** client tax returns, bank statements, payroll data, ID documents. All of it is personal or financial data.
**Systems:** Microsoft 365 (email, files), a cloud accounting platform, a shared scanner/printer, staff phones with email installed.
**Regulation:** UK GDPR. The firm is regulated by its professional accountancy body.

## Engagement
- **You are:** an outside assessor doing a quick review for the partners, who want to know how they're doing before a client questionnaire arrives.
- **Two topics only:** (1) who has access to what, and (2) how the firm handles security incidents.
- **Audit period:** 1 Jan to 30 Sep 2026.
- **Evidence:** in `data-room/`. Ask the client for anything else you need (in chat).
- **Method:** compare what the firm's policies say with what the evidence shows. The gaps are where the two don't match.

## Your output
Five findings in the four-line format in `my-work/findings-template.md`. Start by reading the two policy documents, then check each against the evidence.
