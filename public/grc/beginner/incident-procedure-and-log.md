# Incident Handling Procedure v1.0

Written: January 2026. Owner: IT lead.

1. Anyone who spots a security problem (suspicious email, lost device, wrong recipient, odd login) tells the IT lead straight away: Priya Shah, ext. 214, priya.shah@haldenmoss.example.
2. The IT lead records the incident in the incident log and takes any immediate action.
3. If client or personal data may be affected, the IT lead tells a partner. The partners decide within 72 hours whether the incident must be reported to the ICO and whether clients must be told.
4. When the incident is closed, the IT lead records the cause in the log.
