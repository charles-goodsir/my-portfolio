# Access Control Policy v1.2

Owner: Office Manager (J. Pryce). Approved by the partners: 3 February 2026. Next review: February 2027.

1. Every member of staff has their own individual account. Shared logins are not allowed.
2. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be switched on for every account.
3. When someone leaves the firm, their account is disabled by the end of their last working day.
4. Access to client files is limited to the staff who work on that client.
